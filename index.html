The garment disappears.
What remains is the silhouette.

A visual study into how clothing creates, distorts, and redefines the human form. Shot against negative space, each look was selected for its architectural quality—tailoring that speaks before the wearer does.

The city is the backdrop.
The street is the studio.

Urban environments are living archives of texture, material, and human mark-making. This series positions fashion within that context—using the city as an equal collaborator in the visual narrative.

Shot over three weeks across multiple districts, the project seeks moments where clothing and architecture share the same visual language: layered, worn, utilitarian yet expressive.

Color as structure.
Tone as narrative.

An editorial investigation into the psychology of color in fashion. Each chapter of the series corresponds to a chromatic temperature—warm, cool, neutral—examining how color changes the perceived weight, season, and mood of a look.

The palette was intentionally desaturated from magazine-vivid tones toward a more archival register. Colors that feel worn-in, lived with—not pristine.

Fashion begins before the body.
It begins in the cloth.

A macro-photography archive dedicated to textile surfaces. Linen, silk, wool, denim, and synthetic blends were photographed at extreme proximity to reveal the inherent beauty of weave structure, fiber direction, and material weight.

The editorial approach treats fabric as landscape—each thread a topographic line, each surface a terrain. No styling. No model. Just material against neutral ground.

These are the images
that didn't fit anywhere else.

A personal archive of fragments—images, moments, and visual notes that resist categorization. Shot on film and digital over several years, Fragments is an ongoing project with no fixed editorial direction. It grows as the work grows.

The project functions as a visual journal: loose, associative, and unresolved. The lack of system is intentional. These images belong together because they were made by the same eyes.
